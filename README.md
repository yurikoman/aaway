# aaway connector

[aaway](https://aaway.de) is a content engine: brand rules, briefs, drafts, human approval and
publishing for social networks. This connector lets **your own AI assistant** (Claude, ChatGPT,
Codex) work in your aaway account: write briefs, review drafts, approve and schedule posts.

## Install

Don't install from this page. Open **https://aaway.de/connect** and follow the steps for your
assistant, or tell your assistant:

> Follow https://aaway.de/connect to connect aaway

If someone invited you, use their personal link (`https://aaway.de/connect/<their-invitation>`):
it carries the invitation to your new account.

## About this repository

This repository is a small placeholder package that some assistants (for example Claude Code)
need in order to install the connector as a plugin. It contains no code and no settings.
The server address, the invitation and the how-to guides are served by aaway.de, so
they can change without you updating anything here.

## What the connector can and cannot do

- You sign in to aaway **in your browser** and click Allow. Your password, cookies and tokens
  are never typed into the chat.
- The assistant works with your brand, briefs, drafts and publishing schedule, with the same
  rights you have in the account.
- Approving a post requires your own words: the assistant must pass what you said, and aaway
  records it.
- It has no access to your wallet, payments, partner program, social network connections or
  administration.
- You can disconnect any assistant at any time at https://aaway.de/app/connect-ai.
